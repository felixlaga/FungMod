# ERLA Product Specification

## Product thesis

ERLA is an epistemic research navigator. It helps researchers move from an unclear research question to an evidence-backed research map, reading plan, gap analysis, and defensible project direction.

ERLA must not become a generic paper-writing autocomplete tool. Writing is a downstream export layer. The core product is research orientation, literature traversal, evidence validation, and research advising.
